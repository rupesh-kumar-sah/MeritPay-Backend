# Support

## Questions and help

- **Usage questions / ideas:** open a
  [GitHub Discussion](https://github.com/MeritPay/MeritPay-Backend/discussions).
- **API contract:** see the `## API` section of the [README](README.md).
- **Running it locally:** see [CONTRIBUTING.md → Development setup](CONTRIBUTING.md#development-setup).

## Bugs and features

- **Bug reports** and **feature requests:** use the
  [issue tracker](https://github.com/MeritPay/MeritPay-Backend/issues) with the provided
  templates.
- **Security vulnerabilities:** do **not** open a public issue — follow
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Related projects

- Circuits, trusted setup, and Soroban contracts:

## Maintenance status

Early-stage / hackathon-origin project. Maintainers review issues and PRs on a
best-effort basis. A response may take a few days.
